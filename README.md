# 📈 Marketing Analytics & Campaign Performance - Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end digital marketing and advertising analytics project tracking ad performance, return on ad spend (ROAS), cost per acquisition (CPA), conversion funnels, device distribution, and keyword optimization. The project features an interactive, multi-page Power BI executive dashboard designed to evaluate marketing campaign efficiency and revenue impact.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Overview Page
* **Core Summary Metrics:**
  * **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** **6.85**[cite: 13]
  * **CPC (Cost Per Click):** **32.69**[cite: 13]
  * **Total Cost:** **538.37K**[cite: 13]
  * **Total Conversions:** **16K**[cite: 13]
  * **Sale Amount:** **4M**[cite: 13]
* **Return on Ad Spend by Ad Name:** Bar chart ranking individual ad IDs (such as A2660, A2585, A3459, etc.) by their respective ROAS efficiency.
* **Yearly Total Cost & Sale Amount:** Time-series line chart comparing advertising spend against generated sales revenue across daily/weekly periods in November.

### 2. Efficiency & Optimization Page
* **Performance Metrics:** Cost Per Click (**1.56**), Average Conversion Rate (**0.05**), Sum of Clicks (**346K**), and Sum of Impressions (**12M**)[cite: 12].
* **Device Performance Table & Breakdown:** Analyzes CPA, conversion rate, and cost across **mobile**, **desktop**, and **tablet** devices. Mobile and tablet devices account for major share distributions in total leads (~34.14%) and conversions (~34.28%)[cite: 12].
* **Funnel Chart:** Visualizes user drop-off across marketing stages—tracking progress from Impressions (**1,151,627**) down to Clicks (**345,725**), Leads (**51,050**), and Conversions (**16,467**)[cite: 12].

### 3. Keywords Deep Dive Page
* **Keyword Performance Table:** Detailed breakdown evaluating search keywords (such as "analytics for data", "data analytics online", "data analytics course", "learn data analytics", etc.) against total clicks, cost, and conversions[cite: 11].
* **ROAS by Keywords:** Bar chart comparing return efficiency across top-performing search terms, maintaining an average benchmark near **6.85**[cite: 11].
* **ROAS by Keywords & Total Cost:** Treemap visualization highlighting ad spend distribution and financial commitment per search keyword (e.g., online data analytic, analytics for data)[cite: 11].
